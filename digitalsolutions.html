Services

Digital Solutions

Custom management tools built around how small businesses actually work — not the other way around.

Many small businesses don't need — or want — complex ERP systems. Their workflows are established, personal, and effective. What they need is a digital layer that respects that way of working while bringing structure, data, and scalability.

My approach

I develop tailored management applications that fit into existing business processes rather than replacing them. The goal is to professionalize day-to-day operations without forcing a complete overhaul of how things are done.

Product & inventory control

Tracking stock, movements and availability in a way that mirrors how the business already manages it — just digitally.

Client management

Organizing client information, history and communications without the overhead of heavy CRM platforms.

Workflow automation

Turning repetitive manual tasks into simple automated processes adapted to each business's routine.

Data & reporting

Giving business owners clear visibility over their numbers — sales, trends, performance — with tools they can actually use.

The result

Businesses gain visibility over their data, can scale their operations, and maintain the essence of how they work.
